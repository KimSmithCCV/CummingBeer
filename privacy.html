Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Cumming Beer LLC ("we," "us") runs this website to share what we're brewing and where to find it in Cumming, Iowa. This page explains what information we collect from visitors, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Site analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site — pages viewed, general location (city/region level, from your IP address), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We don't see your name or exact identity through this data.
  • eNews signup. If you subscribe to our email list, we collect the email address you provide. This is submitted through a third-party form service (FormSubmit.co) and used only to send you updates about new releases.
  • Instagram feed. Our "On Instagram" section is powered by a third-party widget (SociableKIT) that pulls public posts from our Instagram account. It doesn't collect information about you.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to distinguish visitors and measure site usage. We don't use cookies for advertising or to sell your data. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time, or use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on to stop Analytics from collecting your data across all sites.

How we use your information

We use analytics data to understand what's working on the site and improve it. We use the email addresses collected through the eNews form only to send occasional updates about new beer releases — we don't sell, rent, or share your email with anyone else. You can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us at the address below.

Age verification

This site asks visitors to confirm they are 21 or older before entering, since our product is alcoholic. We don't store or transmit your birthdate or any identifying information from this step — it's a simple yes/no confirmation saved locally in your browser.

Third parties

We use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy practices: Google Analytics, FormSubmit.co (eNews form delivery), SociableKIT (Instagram feed embed), and Instagram/Meta (for our embedded feed and linked profile).

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at Info@CummingBeer.com.
